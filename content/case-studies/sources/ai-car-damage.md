# Background
EIA Scan is an AI-powered platform that provides fast and accurate car damage assessment using photos and videos.
<callout icon="💡" color="purple_bg">
	The product **simplifies** insurance claims, repair cost estimation, and vehicle inspections, reducing the evaluation process** from days to minutes**.
</callout>
<columns>
	<column>
		# Challenge
	</column>
	<column>
		# Goal
	</column>
</columns>
<columns>
	<column ratio="50">
		<callout color="gray_bg">
			- Insurance companies spend too much time on manual assessments.
			- Existing tools are fragmented, often requiring in-person inspections.
			- Car owners lack a transparent way to understand the scale of damage.
			- The market needs an automated solution that generates industry-accepted PDF reports.
		</callout>
	</column>
	<column ratio="50">
		<callout color="gray_bg">
			- Create a seamless digital flow that allows users to submit accident information in **under 5 minutes**.
			- Design a **professional and reliable system **that generates PDF reports trusted by both insurers and repair shops.
			- Ensure the product works equally well on **desktop and mobile** for different user groups.
		</callout>
	</column>
</columns>

		# Learnings

<columns>
	<column ratio="50">
		<callout color="gray_bg">
			- Reduced assessment time from \~3 days to under 1 hour
			- Increased accuracy and trust with AI-powered evaluation
			- Error rate in form submissions significantly decreased thanks to contextual guidance
		</callout>
	</column>
	<column ratio="50">
		<callout color="gray_bg">
			- **UX writing matters:** contextual instructions dramatically reduced mistakes
			- **Less is more:** reducing report flow to 4 steps improved conversion rates
			- **Mobile-first behavior:** most users submitted photos directly from the accident scene, making responsive design critical
		</callout>
	</column>
