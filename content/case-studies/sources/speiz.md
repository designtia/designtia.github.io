# Background
Back in the day, when to-be Speiz team was on the hunt for a warehouse, they felt like the industry is analog, outdated, and full of traditional barriers. What they expected to be a straightforward process turned into endless scrolling through confusing listings, unclear terms, and constant back-and-forth with multiple middlemen.
Frustrated by their own struggles, the team had a light bulb moment: What if leasing a warehouse could be as simple as booking a hotel room?
<callout icon="💡" color="purple_bg">
	That's the spark that ignited Speiz. The team decided to design a platform to spare businesses the headache they went through. 
</callout>
