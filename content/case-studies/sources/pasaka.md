# About the project
The Mokyklėlė “Pasaka” platform is an online learning system designed to support Lithuanian language education for children and families worldwide. It delivers immersive language education— not only to help kids engage with Lithuanian, but also to support their parents in understanding multilingualism.
<callout icon="💡" color="purple_bg">
	The platform was built to handle **different user roles**, each with their own interface and workflows, while ensuring **smooth communication** between learners, teachers, and administrators.
</callout>

# **Design Approach**
The system was designed with three key principles in mind:
<callout color="gray_bg">
	**Clarity**
	Each role has a dedicated interface tailored to its needs, reducing complexity and confusion
</callout>
<callout color="gray_bg">
	**Scalability**
	Parents can manage multiple children across different courses; administrators can handle dozens of teachers and hundreds of students efficiently
</callout>
<callout color="gray_bg">
	**Consistency** 
	Unified design language ensures that whether a parent, teacher, or admin logs in, the platform feels cohesive, friendly, and reliable
</callout>
