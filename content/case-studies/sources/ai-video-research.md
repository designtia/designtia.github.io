# Background
Traditional surveys are often long, impersonal, and ineffective. Respondents experience fatigue, while companies struggle with low completion rates and shallow insights. 
<callout icon="💡" color="purple_bg">
	Insightvid set out to **redefine the feedback loop **by introducing **video-first surveys**: human, conversational, and designed to capture richer insights.
</callout>
# Challenge
We faced two core challenges:
<columns>
	<column ratio="100">
		<callout color="gray_bg">
			**For respondents**:
			- Surveys felt transactional, boring, and time-consuming.
			- Respondents rarely felt their feedback was valued.
			- Video added friction: people could feel exposed or hesitant.
		</callout>
		<empty-block/>
	</column>
	<column ratio="100">
		<callout color="gray_bg">
			**For companies**:
			- Existing survey tools delivered quantitative data but lacked emotional depth.
			- They needed **actionable insights** from authentic human responses.
			- The survey experience had to reflect positively on their brand.
		</callout>
	</column>
</columns>
# Goal
<callout icon="🎯" color="purple_bg">
	To design a seamless, video-based survey experience that works for both sides.
</callout>
<columns>
	<column ratio="50">
		<callout color="gray_bg">
			- Create a survey flow that feels human, short, and rewarding for respondents.
			- Give companies high-quality, emotionally rich insights.
		</callout>
	</column>
	<column ratio="50">
		<callout color="gray_bg">
			- Balance simplicity with flexibility (scalable across different brands and industries).
			- Ensure transparency, control, and trust at every step.
		</callout>
	</column>
</columns>
# Company experience
For companies, the design had to be more than a “survey builder” — it had to be an **insight engine**.
<callout color="gray_bg">
	**Key features included:**
	- **Customizable surveys** with brand elements
	- **Video responses dashboard** with easy review, tagging, and exporting
	- **AI-assisted summaries** with not just raw video, but structured summaries and sentiment markers
	- **Scalability** to make the solution adaptable to industries from retail to tech
</callout>
