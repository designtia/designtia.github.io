# Background
A company specializing in custom commercial vehicle engineering, building everything from mobile labs to high-end service vans, needed a stronger digital presence. Their expertise spans modular shelving, lighting, electronics integration, and certification, but their website didn’t yet reflect the scale of their work or support part sales online.
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	They wanted a way to showcase their craftsmanship while opening a new channel for selling custom-made components.
</callout>
# Challenge
While their engineering solutions were well recognized, the digital experience lagged behind. The business needed:
<callout color="gray_bg">
	- A modern landing page that communicates expertise and showcases project examples
	- An e-shop to sell custom-made parts directly to clients
	- A conversion-focused structure to turn interest into sales and inquiries
</callout>
# Goals
The goal was to design a digital platform that combines **storytelling with functionality**: a landing page to present complex engineering solutions with clarity, and an e-commerce storefront tailored to selling specialized parts in a professional, user-friendly way.
# Solution
We built a dual-purpose platform:
<callout color="gray_bg">
	**The landing page** highlights engineering projects, service capabilities, and a clear value proposition structured to guide visitors naturally toward exploring parts and solutions.
